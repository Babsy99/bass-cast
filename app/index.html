Before you scan

  1. Have your iPhone unlocked, on any network (same Wi-Fi is fastest, not required).
  2. When the code appears, open the iPhone Camera, point it at the code, tap the link.
  3. Tap Allow motion and connect, then Allow on the motion prompt.
  4. Casting: hold the phone upright like a rod handle, screen facing you. Swing it back over your shoulder, then flick forward. That's it — no button needed. Holding the button is optional (let go to release).
  5. A relaxed wrist flick is a normal cast. You never need to throw hard: anything over 500°/s is "too hard" in the test.

The pairing clock starts when you press Show QR and stops at your first cast.
